# LAFAms – Project Overview & Requirements

**Project Name:** LAFAms (LAFAMTAT)  
**Project Type:** Financial Management and Accounting System  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Development Status:** Development / MVP  
**Business Model:** Single financial institution, single operational branch  
**Primary Goal:** Digitize and manage the institution's existing handwritten-register and physical-receipt-based financial operations.

---

## 1. Project Overview

LAFAms is a centralized financial management system designed to digitize the daily operations of a financial institution.

The system replaces manual registers, paper-based customer records, physical transaction tracking, and scattered financial documentation with a centralized, secure, customer-oriented digital platform.

The system must maintain accurate customer records, financial activity histories, staff accountability, administrative control, and secure access to information.

### Core Principles

1. Customer-centered architecture: One customer profile linked to all financial products and activities.
2. Single institution: No multi-branch management in the current scope.
3. Security first: Authentication, authorization, validation, auditing, and protected documents.
4. Data integrity: Financial records must maintain consistent relationships and transaction histories.
5. Administrative control: Critical business rules and decisions remain under Admin authority.
6. Reusable architecture: Maintain modular, maintainable, and reusable code.
7. Practical development: Build necessary functionality without unnecessary complexity.
8. Traceability: Important activities must be attributable to the responsible user.

## 3. Functional Requirements

### 3.1 Authentication and User Management
- Secure login and logout.
- Session-based authentication.
- Role-based access.
- Account activation and deactivation.
- Password setup and reset.
- Staff account management.
- Customer portal account management.
- Only one Admin account is permitted.
- Customer self-registration is not allowed.

### 3.2 Customer Management
- Register customers.
- Maintain a centralized customer profile.
- Store personal information and contact details.
- Store required citizenship/KYC documents and profile photographs.
- Update customer information through authorized workflows.
- Track KYC verification status.
- View customer-related financial records.
- Support inactive customer records without destroying financial history.

### 3.3 Loan Management
- Register and manage loans.
- Link loans to existing customers.
- Store principal, rate, dates, and other required loan information.
- Maintain loan history.
- Record repayments.
- Maintain repayment history.
- Support repayment correction through an authorized process.
- Require Admin approval for restricted and backdated repayment changes.

**MVP limitation:** Loan interest calculations, outstanding balance calculations, penalty calculations, and repayment allocation are not implemented. The loan rate is stored as entered.

### 3.4 Gold and Collateral Management
- Register gold collateral.
- Link collateral to customers and relevant loans.
- Record gold description, weight, valuation, and other approved details.
- Maintain collateral records and history.
- Support authorized updates and status changes.

### 3.5 Deposit Management

**Current Account**
- Create and manage current accounts.
- Link accounts to customers.
- Record deposits and withdrawals where authorized.
- Maintain transaction history.

**Fixed Deposit**
- Create and manage fixed deposits.
- Store principal, duration, rate, and maturity information.
- Maintain FD records.

**MVP limitation:** Current-account interest posting, FD interest calculation, and FD withdrawal processing are not implemented.

### 3.6 Financial Operations
- Record incoming and outgoing transactions.
- Maintain transaction references.
- Record remittance sent and received.
- Maintain payment and repayment histories.
- Manage inventory records.
- Maintain transaction accountability.

### 3.7 Staff Management
- Admin creates and manages staff accounts.
- Assign roles and privileges.
- Activate and deactivate staff.
- Control staff permissions.
- Track staff activities.
- Revoke existing sessions when staff access is changed.

### 3.8 Approval Management
- Staff submits restricted update requests.
- Admin reviews pending requests.
- Admin approves or rejects requests.
- Approved changes are applied through authorized workflows.
- Maintain approval history.

### 3.9 Dashboard and Notifications

**Admin dashboard priority:**
1. Company overview.
2. Today's activities.
3. Pending tasks and approvals.
4. Financial and operational summaries.
5. Notifications and recent activities.

**Staff dashboard:**
- Assigned activities.
- Customer management.
- Transaction activities.
- Pending update requests.
- Notifications.

**Customer dashboard:**
- Own profile.
- KYC status.
- Loans and collateral.
- Deposit information.
- Payment and transaction history.

### 3.10 Audit Logging
Maintain records of important activities, including:
- User login and logout.
- Customer creation and modification.
- Loan creation and modification.
- Repayment recording and correction.
- Account activation and deactivation.
- Permission changes.
- Approval and rejection.
- Administrative actions.

### 3.11 Reports
- Customer reports.
- Loan records.
- Repayment history.
- Deposit records.
- Transaction history.
- Operational summaries.

**MVP limitation:** Report export functionality is not implemented.

### 3.12 Customer Portal
- No public customer registration.
- Accounts are created by authorized staff.
- Customer access requires an active account.
- Customers can view only their own information.
- Customer portal is read-only.
- Customer ownership must be verified on the backend, not through URL parameters alone.

## 4. Non-Functional Requirements

### Security
- Passwords must never be stored as plain text.
- Validate all incoming requests.
- Enforce backend authorization.
- Protect customer documents.
- Secure sessions and cookies.
- Apply CSRF protection.
- Protect authentication endpoints against brute-force attempts.
- Maintain audit logs.
- Keep secrets outside source control.

### Performance
- Use database indexing where appropriate.
- Avoid unnecessary database queries.
- Use pagination for large records.
- Keep frontend components reusable.
- Optimize image uploads and retrieval.

### Reliability
- Use database transactions for related financial operations.
- Prevent accidental duplicate financial records.
- Maintain referential integrity.
- Provide database backup and recovery procedures.

### Maintainability
- Use modular architecture.
- Prefer ES modules.
- Use named exports.
- Use `const` and arrow functions.
- Follow camelCase naming.
- Add useful comments where necessary.
- Keep business logic separate from controllers and routes.
- Avoid duplicated code.

## 5. Important Business Constraints

- The institution operates as one centralized organization.
- Only one Admin account is allowed.
- Customers must have one centralized profile.
- Financial history must not be casually deleted.
- Historical records should be closed or marked inactive instead of deleted.
- Staff cannot override Admin-controlled decisions.
- Customers cannot modify financial records.
- Business rules must be approved by the institution before implementation.
- Do not invent interest rates, penalties, approval thresholds, or regulatory policies.

## 6. MVP Scope vs Future Development

### Current MVP Scope
- Authentication and user lifecycle.
- Customer management.
- Role and permission management.
- Loan CRUD.
- Repayment recording.
- Gold and collateral management.
- Current accounts.
- Fixed deposits.
- Staff management.
- Approval workflows.
- Audit logs.
- Notifications.
- Customer portal.
- Encrypted backup and restore.

### Known MVP Limitations
- Admin two-factor authentication is deferred.
- Loan interest and outstanding balance calculations are disabled.
- Current-account interest posting is disabled.
- FD interest and withdrawal processing are disabled.
- Report export is not implemented.
- Database-backed restore rehearsal has not yet passed in the current development environment.

### Future Scope
- Financial calculations after policy approval.
- Advanced reporting and export.
- Admin two-factor authentication.
- Improved analytics.
- Automated operational reports.
- Additional security and compliance improvements.

## 7. Project Development Philosophy

The project owner makes all important business and architectural decisions.

AI acts as a technical assistant, developer, reviewer, and documentation assistant.

AI must not independently invent financial policies or change approved business requirements.

Development must proceed incrementally:
1. Define requirements.
2. Design database structure.
3. Implement backend functionality.
4. Implement frontend functionality.
5. Integrate frontend and backend.
6. Test functionality and permissions.
7. Harden security.
8. Prepare staging.
9. Complete production verification.
10. Deploy and monitor.
